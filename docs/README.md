# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README is the entry point to OctoAcme's project management processes and guides.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative approach to deliver projects:

- **Initiation:** Validate the need and success measures, identify stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed to planning.
- **Planning:** Prioritize and estimate work, define acceptance criteria, and agree on milestones, dependencies, and risks.
- **Execution & tracking:** Deliver in small increments, track progress on the project board, and review work through team syncs and demos.
- **Risk management & communication:** Maintain and review risks regularly, share stakeholder updates, and escalate blockers through clear paths.
- **Release & deployment:** Confirm release readiness, deploy and verify changes, communicate releases, and keep a rollback plan available.
- **Retrospective & continuous improvement:** Capture lessons after milestones, assign improvement actions, and follow up on their impact.
- **Roles & personas:** Clarify responsibilities across project and product managers, developers, QA, and stakeholders.
